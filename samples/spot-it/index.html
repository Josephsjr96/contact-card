How to play

Find the one symbol shared by your card and the table card. Drag it onto its twin, or select your symbol, then its twin. You can also use Tab and Enter.

First to 20 wins. Escape pauses solo play. For PvP, choose 2 players, enter a name and share your room link. Both players press Ready. The server awards each round to the first correct match it receives. The match pauses if a player disconnects.
